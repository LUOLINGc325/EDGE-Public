# A square-to-clipped-square transition in high-privacy noise

**高隐私极限下从正方形到削角正方形的最优噪声**

For planar location release with Manhattan sensitivity and Euclidean power loss, the limiting optimal additive noise is uniform on a square for powers at most two and on a square clipped by a concentric disk for larger powers.

对曼哈顿距离灵敏度下的二维位置发布，论文求出了高隐私极限中的最优加性噪声。欧氏损失的幂次不超过 2 时最优形状为正方形，超过 2 时由同心圆盘切去正方形的四角。
