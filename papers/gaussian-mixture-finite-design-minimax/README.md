# Gaussian mixing recovery under response-dependent repeat measurements

**高斯混合分布在依响应复测下的最优恢复速度**

The paper characterizes the joint minimax Wasserstein rate for a compact distribution of means and variances. Initial gains come from a finite menu. The design may use previous responses to choose whom to remeasure and at what gain, with a total repeat budget and a fixed cap on views per individual.

论文求出紧支撑高斯均值与方差联合分布的最小极大 Wasserstein 收敛阶。初测增益来自有限列表。设计可以根据已有响应选择复测对象与增益，同时受复测总预算和每人固定测量次数上限约束。
