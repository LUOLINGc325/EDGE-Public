# Sharp finite-size inefficiency on common-slope exponential parallel links

**同斜率指数并行路由的精确有限规模拥塞代价**

For atomic splittable congestion games with common-slope exponential parallel costs, the paper computes the worst price of anarchy at fixed numbers of users and links, and characterizes load configurations approaching it after reduction to equal demands.

对同斜率指数费用的并行链路和可分流的原子拥塞博弈，论文求出固定参与者与链路数量下的最坏无政府代价，并在归约为等需求后刻画接近极值的负载配置。
