# Exact feedback thresholds and critical scaling in compound-geometric FIFO timing channels

**复合几何 FIFO 信道的精确反馈门槛与临界尺度**

For discrete FIFO timing channels with compound-geometric service in the regime where a geometric output attains full-feedback capacity, the paper finds the smallest alarm threshold at a fixed output rate. The threshold is infinite at the critical endpoint. A Gamma scaling law holds under the stated regular-variation hypothesis on the critical tilted jump law.

在几何输出分布可以达到全反馈容量的复合几何 FIFO 时序信道中，论文求出给定输出速率下保留全反馈容量的最小告警阈值。临界端点需要无限阈值。临界倾斜跳跃分布满足文中的正则变化条件时，可得到 Gamma 缩放极限。
