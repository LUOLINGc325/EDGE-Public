# Persistence exponents for ordered families of Brownian and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck walkers

**有序布朗与 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 粒子的持续性指数**

The paper proves Brownian and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck persistence asymptotics for ordered particle families, including an Airy term and a macroscopic spectral partition. Its open TASEP consequence is explicitly conditional on a transfer hypothesis.

论文证明有序布朗与 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 粒子族的持续性渐近式，包括 Airy 修正项和宏观谱分割。文中的开放 TASEP 推论明确依赖传递假设。
