# Exact Simes calibration under finite-order independence

**有限阶独立条件下 Simes 检验的精确校准**

The paper derives sharp small-level calibration of the Simes global-null test under r-wise independence. It also gives the sharp calibration under independence through degree r−1 and a degree-r factorial-moment bound at every common threshold. Matching constructions distinguish the two leading size corrections.

论文给出 r 阶独立下 Simes 全局零假设检验的尖锐小水平校准。若独立性只到 r−1 阶，同时对每个共同阈值施加 r 阶阶乘矩上界，则得到另一精确校准。两类首项修正都有达到界限的分布构造。
