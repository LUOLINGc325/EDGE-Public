# Pointwise minimax rates for sampled birth–death trees under vanishing intervention contrast

**两组抽样出生–死亡树的最优估计速度**

For two labelled samples of reconstructed stochastic trees with a known contrast between birth multipliers, the oldest visible split and arm achieve the full-tree minimax pointwise rates for both birth and death as the contrast vanishes.

在两组带标签的重建随机树中，若两组出生倍率之差已知，使用分组和最早可见分支即可达到利用整棵树估计出生率与死亡率的最小极大点态速度，包括倍率差趋零的情形。
